# A+ content brief - Skincare Brand Hyaluronic Acid Serum (US)

## M1 - Brand hero banner
Purpose: stop the scroll and state the positioning.
Creative direction: full-width banner in deep green fading to soft aqua, bottle right of centre with a single water ripple beneath it. Clean and botanical, with generous empty space on the left for the headline.
Headline: Hydration, Simplified
Subline: A water-light serum for skin that looks plump and dewy
Alt text: Skincare Brand hyaluronic acid serum bottle on a green and aqua background

## M2 - Hero ingredient spotlight
Purpose: explain the hero active in plain words.
Creative direction: split layout. Left, a water-droplet macro inside a soft circular frame, standing for a humectant. Right, bottle and carton on white.
Header: Hyaluronic Acid, Lasting Soft Feel
Line 1: A humectant that draws moisture to the surface of your skin.
Line 2: Skin feels hydrated and looks smooth, bouncy and fresh every day.
Alt text: Water droplet macro next to a hydrating face serum with hyaluronic acid

## M3 - Formula story
Purpose: cover the full formula in three benefit pillars.
Creative direction: three panels with a deep green header band and aqua accent stripe. Panel 1 texture macro, panel 2 model applying the serum, panel 3 bottle on a pale stone plinth.
Panel 1 - Hydrate:
- **Hyaluronic Acid** - draws moisture to the surface for a supple, hydrated feel
- **Hydrolyzed Hyaluronic Acid** - a lightweight form that supports a plump, dewy look
Panel 2 - Comfort:
- **Pro-Vitamin B5** - hydrates and comforts so skin feels soft and smooth
- **Aloe Vera** - helps soothe for a calm, refreshed look
Panel 3 - Protect the glow:
- **Vitamin E** - supports the look of skin against daily environmental stressors
- **Green Tea Extract** - antioxidant-rich care for a fresh, healthy-looking complexion
Alt text: Three panels showing the ingredients in a moisturizing serum for face

## M4 - Why this formula
Purpose: three plain reasons to choose it, stated as facts about this product.
Creative direction: bottle centred on white with three short statements around it, each with a green check icon. No other products in frame.
- Two forms of hyaluronic acid
- Clear gel, absorbs in seconds
- Simple enough for a first serum
Alt text: Hyaluronic acid serum for dry skin with three formula benefits

## M5 - Routine how-to
Purpose: show where it fits and lower the barrier for first-time serum users.
Creative direction: warm bathroom scene in natural light, model pressing serum into her cheek. A deep green step bar runs along the bottom with white numerals.
Header: Four Steps to Dewy Skin
Step 1: Cleanse
Step 2: Apply to damp skin
Step 3: Press in gently
Step 4: Seal with moisturizer
Note: Use morning and evening.
Alt text: Woman applying a lightweight face serum as part of a daily routine

## M6 - Range comparison chart
Purpose: help the shopper choose within the range and raise basket size.
Creative direction: standard comparison chart module, product packshots on white, this serum in the first column.

| Product | Choose it for | Key ingredient | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hyaluronic Acid Serum | Hydration, plump look | Hyaluronic Acid | AM and PM |
| Vitamin C Serum | Brighter-looking tone | Vitamin C | AM |
| Niacinamide Serum | Look of pores, balanced tone | Niacinamide | AM or PM |
| Retinol Serum | Smoother-looking texture | Retinol | PM |

> Production note: confirm ASINs and hero claims for the three sister products against their own Product Bibles before upload.

## M7 - Brand values
Purpose: close with verified brand ethics.
Creative direction: white background, two circular stamp badges in deep green with white line art, centred. No lifestyle imagery.
Header: Kind to Skin, Kind by Design
Badge 1: Cruelty Free
Badge 2: Vegan
Closing line: Ingredient-led skincare that everyone can afford to love.
Alt text: Cruelty free and vegan badges for Skincare Brand skincare
