# Gallery image brief - Skincare Brand Hyaluronic Acid Serum (US)

Formula: clear, water-light gel. Palette: deep green #1F4D3A with soft aqua #BFE3E0 accent.

## Main image
Purpose: show exactly what arrives.
Visual: bottle and carton on pure white, front-facing, filling about 85% of the frame. No text, badges or props.
> Production note: use the final artwork photo, not a render of an earlier label.

## G1 - Ingredients
Purpose: show what is inside and why it matters.
Visual: bottle centred on soft aqua. Hero ingredient as a large water-droplet macro on the left; supporting and botanical ingredients as small circular insets on the right.
On-image copy:
**Hyaluronic Acid** A moisture magnet that leaves skin looking plump, smooth and dewy.
**Pro-Vitamin B5** Soft, comfortable feel.
**Hydrolyzed Hyaluronic Acid** Lightweight hydration for a plump look.
**Vitamin E** Supports skin against daily stressors.
**Aloe Vera** Calm, refreshed-looking skin.
**Green Tea Extract** Fresh, healthy-looking complexion.
Botanicals: Aloe Vera | Green Tea Extract | Jojoba Oil

## G2 - Core benefit
Purpose: one promise, readable at thumbnail size.
Visual: close crop of a model's cheek with a visible dewy finish, dropper entering frame top right. Natural daylight, minimal retouching.
On-image copy:
Headline: Plump, Dewy Skin
Subline: Daily hydration that feels like a glass of water

## G3 - Benefits infographic
Purpose: five reasons to buy, scannable in one pass.
Visual: bottle left, five line icons stacked right on white, green icon strokes.
On-image copy:
Headline: Hydration Made Simple
- **Lasting Moisture** - skin feels soft all day
- **Plump Look** - smooths the look of dry lines
- **Water-Light Gel** - absorbs in seconds, never sticky
- **Layers Easily** - sits well under makeup and sunscreen
- **Everyday Gentle** - made for morning and evening use

## G4 - Routine
Purpose: show where the serum fits and introduce the range.
Visual: two rows labelled AM and PM, product bottles in order of use, this serum highlighted with an aqua ring.
On-image copy:
- **AM: Vitamin C Serum + Hyaluronic Acid Serum** for a bright, dewy start
- **PM: Retinol Serum + Hyaluronic Acid Serum** for a comfortable finish
- **Any time: Niacinamide Serum** layers before this serum
> Production note: keep Vitamin C Serum and Retinol Serum in separate rows; they are not shown in the same routine.

## G5 - How to use
Purpose: remove any doubt about application.
Visual: four numbered square frames, hands and face only.
On-image copy:
Step 1: Cleanse and leave skin slightly damp
Step 2: Apply 3-4 drops to face and neck
Step 3: Press in gently with fingertips
Step 4: Follow with moisturizer
Note: For external use only.

## G6 - Texture
Purpose: let the shopper feel the formula.
Visual: macro of a clear gel drop spreading on skin, backlit so it catches light; bottle out of focus behind.
On-image copy:
Headline: Clear. Light. Never Sticky.
> Production note: texture image chosen instead of before-and-after. No substantiated results photography is on file, so no results imagery is used.

## G7 - Brand values
Purpose: close with what the brand stands for.
Visual: two round stamp-style badges in deep green on white, evenly spaced, bottle small at centre.
On-image copy:
- Cruelty Free
- Vegan
> Production note: only the two claims in the register are shown. Add further badges only once they are documented.
