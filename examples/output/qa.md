# Shopper Q&A - Skincare Brand Hyaluronic Acid Serum (US)

**Q1: Can I use hyaluronic acid serum every day?**

A1: Yes. This serum is made for daily use, morning and evening. Apply 3-4 drops to clean, damp skin and follow with moisturizer to help keep that hydrated feel.

**Q2: Is hyaluronic acid serum good for sensitive skin?**

A2: The formula is built around hyaluronic acid, Pro-Vitamin B5 and Aloe Vera, which are known for a comfortable, soothing feel. Every skin is different, so if yours is reactive we suggest a patch test on the inner arm before using it on your face.

**Q3: Can I use hyaluronic acid with vitamin C or retinol?**

A3: Yes, it layers well with both. Use a vitamin C serum in the morning before this one, and apply this serum after retinol in the evening for a more comfortable feel. We suggest keeping vitamin C and retinol in separate routines.

**Q4: Do I need a moisturizer after hyaluronic acid serum?**

A4: We recommend it. Hyaluronic acid draws moisture to the skin surface, and a moisturizer on top helps hold that hydration in so skin stays soft and supple for longer.

**Q5: Is this serum safe to use during pregnancy?**

A5: The serum contains hyaluronic acid, panthenol, vitamin E and botanical extracts, and no retinol or exfoliating acids. Because every pregnancy is different, please check the full ingredient list with your doctor before use.
