# Product Bible - Skincare Brand Hyaluronic Acid Serum | 1 fl oz (30 ml) dropper bottle | US

## Summary
A lightweight daily serum with hyaluronic acid and pro-vitamin B5 for skin that looks plump, smooth and dewy. The clear, water-light gel absorbs quickly and layers easily under moisturizer and makeup. Gentle enough for everyday use, it suits dry, normal and combination skin and anyone starting a hydration routine.

## Key ingredients
- Hyaluronic Acid (Sodium Hyaluronate): A humectant that draws moisture to the skin surface for a hydrated, supple feel.

## Supporting ingredients
- Pro-Vitamin B5 (Panthenol): Hydrates and comforts the skin for a soft, smooth feel.
- Hydrolyzed Hyaluronic Acid (Hydrolyzed Sodium Hyaluronate): A lightweight form of hyaluronic acid that supports a plump, hydrated look.
- Vitamin E (Tocopherol): Helps support the skin's appearance against environmental stressors.

## Botanical highlights
- Aloe Vera (Aloe Barbadensis Leaf Juice): Helps soothe and hydrate for a calm, refreshed look.
- Green Tea Extract (Camellia Sinensis Leaf Extract): Antioxidant-rich botanical care for a fresh, healthy-looking complexion.

## Full botanical list
Aloe Vera | Green Tea Extract | Jojoba Oil

## Target customer
Dry, normal and combination skin that feels tight or looks dull. Shoppers new to serums who want one simple hydrating step; also layers under stronger actives for experienced users.

## Verified claims
Cruelty Free, Vegan (from the claims register).

## Routine and cross-sell
- Vitamin C Serum: morning, before this serum, for a brighter-looking start to the day.
- Niacinamide Serum: morning or evening, layers well with hyaluronic acid.
- Retinol Serum: evening only; apply this serum afterwards for a comfortable feel. Do not layer Retinol Serum and Vitamin C Serum in the same routine.

## Prohibited for this product
No "fragrance free", "dermatologist tested" or "hypoallergenic" (not in the claims register). No concentration percentage on the front end.
