# Skincare Brand - brand brief (fictional example)

**Positioning:** ingredient-led skincare that stays approachable. Affordable, inclusive, never clinical or intimidating.
**Voice:** warm, plain-spoken, science-aware. Speaks to what the customer wants for their skin, not to their insecurities.
**Palette:** deep green (#1F4D3A) as the brand base, one accent colour per product line (this serum: soft aqua #BFE3E0).
**Fixed brand claims:** Cruelty Free, Vegan.
**Range (for routines and cross-sell):** Hyaluronic Acid Serum, Vitamin C Serum, Retinol Serum, Niacinamide Serum.

## Packaging copy - Hyaluronic Acid Serum
A lightweight daily serum with hyaluronic acid and pro-vitamin B5 for skin that looks plump, smooth and dewy.
Directions: apply 3-4 drops to clean, damp skin morning and evening. Follow with moisturizer. For external use only.
